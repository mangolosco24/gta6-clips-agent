## Lessons so far
- First comment = a genuine question on the moment ("how did YOU react to this scene?"), never
  "comment below"-style bait (owner's idea, 2026-11-24): track if comments go up.
- TikTok @clip6_gtavi audience 2026-11-24: Italy 79%, France 10.6%, Switzerland 1.7%, UK <1%:
  Tier 1 ~1%, far below the 40% the campaigns require. Do NOT submit TikTok links until this changes
  (rejected clips also fill the pending-clip limit). YouTube country split still to check.
- TEST IN PROGRESS: upload YouTube Shorts by hand (phone app) instead of publisher.py and compare.
- First reactions / opinions about GTA 6 itself (rating, "is it worth it", first impressions)
  did best: launch week, people search what streamers think of the game. Prefer them while the
  game is new.
- 2026-11-19 23:30 (2.8k total): the 3 Shorts with 500-850 views (rates_10, silent_disco, gas_price) are all
  short (22-30 s), cold open on a clear in-game moment with a funny/opinion line. crib_renovation (38.7 s,
  slow start: "for my baby girl...") got only 10 views in 6 h: avoid clips over 30 s and slow openings.
- IRL / non-gameplay (empty_box) does not travel: 25 views. Keep to GTA 6 gameplay + reaction.
- Views are paid per video above a threshold (Tomo 2k, Dom 5k, Dylan 10k): one strong Short beats many
  average ones. Prefer fewer, stronger moments over filling slots.
- Not proven yet whether time or content made the difference: compare with the 20:00 and 23:00 posts.
