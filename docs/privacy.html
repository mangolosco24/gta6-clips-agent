Privacy Policy - GTA 6 Clips Publisher

Last updated: November 18, 2026

GTA 6 Clips Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to manage comments on it.

Data accessed

With the user's consent, the app requests the Google OAuth scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (to upload videos with their title, description and tags to the authorized channel) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (to read and reply to comments on the authorized channel's own videos). It does not access any other Google data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties, and no analytics or tracking are used.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions: write to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
